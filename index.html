Abstract

NVTalk is an audio-driven talking-head framework that generates photo-realistic, temporally coherent facial animations from a single still image and an arbitrary speech audio. The model disentangles identity, head pose, and lip motion, enabling fine-grained control over the synthesized video. Building upon the advances of VASA-1 (diffusion-based holistic motion), LivePortrait (retargeting-based portrait animation), and Ditto (identity-preserving talking head), NVTalk introduces a unified pipeline that achieves high fidelity with low inference latency.
